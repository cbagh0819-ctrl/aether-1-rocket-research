# AETHER-1 | Blender concept prototype

AETHER-1 is an editable Blender concept model for a hydrogen-fed nuclear-thermal rocket engine. The geometry includes the vehicle interface, shielded engine housing, hydrogen feed lines, gimbal support, and a vacuum expansion nozzle. Individual parts are named in the Blender scene. The timeline includes a 120-frame looping, three-layer exhaust flicker; press Spacebar in Blender to play it.

## Efficiency target

The render labels **~50% less propellant mass** as a design target for a defined mission and chemical-engine baseline. It is not a measured or simulated result. NASA describes nuclear-thermal propulsion as having potential for about twice the specific impulse of the best chemical engines, but that does not translate directly into half the propellant for every mission. Required propellant depends on mission delta-v, thrust, staging, vehicle dry mass, tankage, and trajectory; lower propellant mass also does not automatically mean lower launch mass. See [NASA's NTP overview](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/space-tech-research-grants/advanced-fuel-forms-for-nuclear-thermal-propulsion-applications/) and [NASA's parametric NTP mission study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230013241).

## Scope

This is a visual 3D prototype for review and editing in Blender. The animated exhaust is a visual effect; it does not simulate combustion or thrust. The scene also does not simulate heat transfer, reactor behavior, structural loads, or propellant flow, and it is not flight hardware. The model intentionally shows only exterior concept geometry.
